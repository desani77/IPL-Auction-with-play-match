About this IPL 2026 Auction & Cricket Match Simulator (Game Guide)

IPL 2026 auction simulator you can play online

This free browser game lets you run an IPL 2026 style auction on your phone or computer, alone or with friends. Take charge of one of the ten franchises, Chennai Super Kings, Mumbai Indians, Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Kolkata Knight Riders, Sunrisers Hyderabad, Gujarat Titans, Rajasthan Royals, Delhi Capitals, Punjab Kings or Lucknow Super Giants, and build a squad with a ₹120 Cr purse. Squads can hold up to 25 players with a maximum of 8 overseas players, so balancing wicketkeepers, batters, all-rounders and bowlers matters.

Mega auction, mini auction, retentions and trades

Start with a full mega auction or the official 2026 squads. Between seasons you can open a trade window to swap players or buy them for cash, then run a mini auction where retained players cost exactly the price you paid for them. Right to Match (RTM) cards, retention limits and purse top-ups keep every season strategic.

T20 and ODI match simulation, series and records

Play a full IPL season with league matches, qualifiers, an eliminator and a final, or take part in ball-by-ball matches with full scorecards. The Quick Match hub offers T20 and 50-over ODI games and bilateral series of one to five matches. These games are kept apart from your season points table and caps, and their records sit in a separate Bilateral & Format Records tab.

Online multiplayer rooms

Create a private room with a secret 8 character code or a public room that appears in the live lobby. Up to ten players can each manage a franchise, chat in the room, and share control through Vice-Host roles. You can customize your franchise name, short code, manager name and logo, and install the game to your home screen. Created by Darshit Desani.
